# Here is a more extensive list of countries that have not been colonized…

Here is a more extensive list of countries that have not been colonized in the traditional sense of being directly controlled and ruled by a foreign power.

## Countries with Independent Histories

### Japan
- Japan remained largely independent throughout its history, with the exception of a brief period of occupation by the United States after World War II.
- Despite being heavily influenced and pressured, Japan was never formally colonized.

### Thailand (formerly Siam)
- Thailand skillfully navigated the pressures of colonial powers in Southeast Asia and managed to avoid direct colonization.
- It maintained its sovereignty through diplomatic negotiations and reforms.

### Liberia
- Founded by the American Colonization Society as a homeland for freed African Americans, Liberia was never formally colonized by a European power.
- Although it did experience significant influence and intervention from the U.S., it was not colonized.

### Ethiopia
- Ethiopia is notable for its successful resistance against Italian colonization during the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
- It was briefly occupied by Italy from 1936 to 1941, but it was not fully colonized.

### Nepal
- Nepal maintained its independence during the British colonial era in India.
- It managed to avoid colonization through strategic diplomacy and military strength.

### San Marino
- San Marino, a small enclave within Italy, claims to be the world’s oldest republic.
- It maintained its independence throughout European history and was not colonized.

### Andorra
- Andorra, nestled between France and Spain, has remained independent and was never colonized.
- Despite its proximity to larger powers, it has preserved its sovereignty.

### Monaco
- Monaco, a tiny city-state on the French Riviera, has preserved its sovereignty and avoided colonization.
- Although it has been a French protectorate, it has never been colonized.

### Liechtenstein
- Liechtenstein, a small principality between Switzerland and Austria, has managed to avoid colonization and remains an independent entity.

### Vatican City
- The Vatican City has remained an independent and sovereign state throughout its history.
- It was not colonized and is governed by the Pope.

## Key Factors in Retaining Sovereignty
These countries have managed to retain their sovereignty through various means, including effective diplomacy, strategic geographic positions, and unique political or historical circumstances.
