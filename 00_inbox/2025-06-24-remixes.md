# Remixes

Distributing remixes as TikTok sounds is a great way to gain exposure. However, when it comes to remixes, there are some legal considerations to keep in mind, especially regarding copyright.

## Platforms for Distributing Remixes

### SoundCloud
- **Usage**: Upload your remixes to SoundCloud and share them on TikTok. SoundCloud has a large remix culture, and many artists share their remixes for public use.
- **Legal Consideration**: Ensure you're not violating any copyright laws. Consider using SoundCloud’s “Creative Commons” licensing options to clarify how others can use your tracks.

### YouTube (via YouTube Music or YouTube Content ID)
- **Usage**: Upload your remix to YouTube. You can then use TikTok’s music library to access the track if YouTube and TikTok have licensing agreements.
- **Legal Consideration**: Some remixes might be blocked or removed if they infringe on copyright. YouTube’s Content ID system flags copyrighted music, so be cautious about what you remix.

### DistroKid
- **Usage**: Distribute music to platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and TikTok using DistroKid. Upload your remixes as long as you have the appropriate permissions or licenses for the original tracks.
- **Legal Consideration**: You need a valid license or permission from the original copyright holder for the remix to be distributed.

### Amuse
- **Usage**: Distribute your remixes to TikTok, Spotify, and other platforms using Amuse. Amuse offers a free option similar to DistroKid.
- **Legal Consideration**: Ensure you have the necessary rights to distribute the remix. Without permission, your remix could be taken down.

### UnitedMasters
- **Usage**: Distribute music to TikTok, Spotify, and other platforms via UnitedMasters. It’s popular among independent artists.
- **Legal Consideration**: Again, you need permission to distribute remixes legally.

### TikTok Sound Upload (via TikTok Music Distribution Partners)
- **Usage**: Some music distribution services have direct partnerships with TikTok (e.g., TuneCore, DistroKid, CD Baby), allowing you to upload music directly to TikTok as a sound.
- **Legal Consideration**: Make sure you have the legal rights to the remix. Some platforms may request proof of your rights to the content you’re distributing.

### Tracklib
- **Usage**: Access original stems and tracks for legal remixing on Tracklib. Remixing tracks available on Tracklib reduces legal risks.
- **Legal Consideration**: Tracks on Tracklib are cleared for remixing. Follow the platform’s rules to stay compliant.

## Tips for Distributing Remixes Legally
- Get permission from the original artist or use royalty-free or Creative Commons music to avoid copyright issues.
- Review the Terms of Service for platforms like TikTok, as they may have specific rules about user-generated content.
- Use services with licensing options to ensure legal coverage when distributing remixes.
- If you're unsure about the copyright of a specific track, it’s safer to reach out to the original creator or obtain a license before distributing your remix.
