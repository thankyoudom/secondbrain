# Playlist Guidelines

A good playlist length depends on the purpose of the playlist. Here are some general guidelines:

## Casual Listening / Daily Use
- **Duration**: 1–2 hours (15–30 songs)
- **Use cases**: Commutes, background music, workouts
- **Note**: Keeps it fresh without feeling repetitive

## Event or Party Playlist
- **Duration**: 3–5 hours (45–75 songs)
- **Purpose**: Long enough to avoid loops and accommodate changing moods or energy levels

## Focused Themes (e.g., Mood, Genre, Artist Highlights)
- **Duration**: 45 minutes to 1.5 hours (10–25 songs)
- **Goal**: Keeps the theme tight and engaging

## Road Trips / Long Listening Sessions
- **Duration**: 5+ hours (75–100+ songs)
- **Use cases**: Extended periods when you don’t want to manually manage music
- **Note**: Too short feels repetitive; too long can be overwhelming unless it’s meant to be shuffled
