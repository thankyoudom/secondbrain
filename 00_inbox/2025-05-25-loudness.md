# LOUDNESS

## Loudness

Got it! It sounds like you're following a workflow where you export your video from CapCut, then extract the audio within TikTok—this could introduce loss in quality and volume. To get the cleanest and loudest track possible from FL Studio all the way to TikTok via CapCut, address key points at each stage.

### 1. Optimize Your FL Studio Output

The first step is ensuring your track is as ready as possible before it hits CapCut.

#### Master to Target Loudness
Aim for an RMS of -10 to -6 dB, not just a peak limit of -1 dB. A low RMS means poor translation through TikTok’s compression.

#### Mastering Chain
Use a limiter (e.g., Fruity Limiter or third-party plugins like iZotope Ozone) to raise loudness without squashing dynamics.  
A multiband compressor can balance the mix—especially if your track has heavy bass or high-frequency content that risks over-compression.

#### Export Settings
Export as a WAV file (not MP3) with:
- 44.1 kHz sample rate (standard for most platforms)
- 24-bit depth for higher resolution (16-bit can also work)
- No dithering in 24-bit
- No compression or lossless codecs (e.g., WAV)

### 2. CapCut Workflow for Audio

Importing into CapCut can affect audio quality.

#### Import WAVs into CapCut
Import the full, lossless WAV file directly instead of a video file. This avoids further compression when audio is extracted in TikTok.

#### Avoid CapCut’s Export Compression
When exporting from CapCut:
- Choose highest-quality settings
- Export at 1080p or -4K resolution (higher resolutions help preserve audio quality)
- Avoid 720p exports, which can lead to noticeable audio degradation

### 3. TikTok Audio Extraction

TikTok’s automatic compression may degrade audio after extraction.

#### Use a Clean Export from CapCut
Avoid extracting audio from low-quality videos in TikTok. If pulled from a compressed CapCut video, the audio may sound flat and under-loud.  
Instead:
- Export your clean WAV (or high-quality MP4) from CapCut
- Upload it as a separate sound in TikTok
- Sync it with your video

#### Upload as a Direct Audio File
Use TikTok’s sound upload feature to upload the audio file directly. This bypasses compression that occurs when extracting audio from a video.

### 4. Handling TikTok’s Compression

TikTok’s compression is inevitable, but you can maximize loudness without sacrificing quality.

#### Maximize Loudness Pre-TikTok
- Aim for peak levels near -1 dB and RMS of -6 to -8 dB
- Test your track in different environments (headphones, car speakers, etc.) to ensure it doesn’t sound squashed

#### Avoid TikTok’s Automatic Enhancements
Turn off any automatic audio enhancements or effects. These can degrade volume and quality.

#### Test Multiple Uploads
Upload different versions:
- A slightly less limited version
- A more “loudness-maximized” version  
This helps you see how much room you have before TikTok applies aggressive compression.

### 5. Additional Tools and Plugins

Recommended tools for better volume control:
- **iZotope Ozone**: A powerful mastering suite with dynamic control—helps maintain punchiness on platforms like TikTok
- **LoudMax Limiter**: A free, transparent limiter that increases loudness without harsh distortion
- **Dynamic EQ**: Tames frequency ranges (e.g., bass or harsh highs) to prevent triggering aggressive compression in TikTok

### Final Workflow Recap

1. **FL Studio**  
   - Master to ~-6 dB RMS (no clipping)  
   - Use a limiter  
   - Export as 24-bit WAV (44.1 kHz)

2. **CapCut**  
   - Import WAV directly  
   - Export at highest resolution and quality

3. **TikTok**  
   - Upload as a direct audio file (not extracted from video)  
   - Preserve best possible quality

With this workflow, you should achieve consistent volume and quality across CapCut and TikTok. Keep tweaking and testing until you find the sweet spot.
