# Who Am I - Feedback

## Short Bio

You’re a multidisciplinary maker: a full-stack developer (React, Node, Python, Firebase, GCP) with a BFA in Graphic Design and hands-on mobile work (Flutter / React Native). You run/ship health-tech projects at Belle IT (RPM, EHR integrations) and build creative work as a music producer inspired by Ivy Lab — all while training as a hybrid athlete and aiming to build a sustainable family life. You hold Salesforce + QlikView certs, you’ve shipped Cloud Functions with Secret Manager, and you’re actively turning technical projects (e.g., a BP-monitor OCR app) into portfolio pieces.

## Philosophical Summary (Short + Honest)

You live at the intersection of two instincts that rarely fit neatly together: the artist’s impulse to refine form and the engineer’s impulse to systematize function. You don’t just want to “make things”; you want those things to be beautiful, reliable, and useful to people (clinics, patients, listeners). That creates a tension: a million interesting directions pull you outward, but your real power comes when you compress that curiosity into repeatable systems — practice routines, templates, deployment pipelines, and feedback loops — so craft becomes leverage. You value clarity, leadership, and real-world impact over pure novelty. The problem to solve for yourself now is: how do you convert varied skills and small projects into credible, compoundable assets (products, reputation, revenue, and a family-ready life)?

## What “Next Level” Looks Like (Concrete Visions)

### Health-Tech Platform Lead (Short-to-Mid Term)

You become the go-to engineer/technical PM who owns the data pipeline for remote patient monitoring: ingestion (mobile OCR), secure storage, analytics, and an operational dashboard used by clinics.

**Signals**:  
- A published case study / pilot with a clinic  
- A repeatable deployment template  
- A documented ROI story that you can cite in sales or hiring conversations  

### Data Scientist / ML-Enabled Product Engineer (12–18 Months)

You convert the BP OCR app into a dataset → pipeline → model. You ship anomaly detection and a lightweight predictive feature (e.g., risk flags) and deploy it on GCP.

**Signals**:  
- Public GitHub project  
- Clean README + demo  
- GCP or Data Eng cert  
- A portfolio piece strong enough to open the doors you want (e.g., entry roles at bigger companies or lead roles at health-tech startups)

### Independent Creative Tech Founder (Parallel Revenue + Brand)

You monetize music/production skills (beat packs, sample subscriptions, sync placements) while using tech skills to scale marketing (automated content pipeline, repurposed short-form clips).

**Signals**:  
- Recurring revenue from music assets  
- Steady growth on TikTok  
- Collaborations/reposts with larger platforms

## Room for Improvement (Non-Generic, Prioritized)

- **Strategic focus**: You spread across many high-value directions. Pick a single “quarterly theme” (one focus for 90 days) and make every project, learning session, and piece of content feed that theme.  
- **Ship-to-learn cadence**: You seem to iterate locally. Shift to a cadence of small public releases: ship a tiny demo every 2 weeks and collect external feedback.  
- **Leverage vs. labor**: You excel at building but under-leverage automation. Turn repetitive work (deploys, demos, social posts) into scripts/CI jobs and templates.  
- **Narrative discipline**: Your story (resume, LinkedIn, QBRs) can better connect craft → impact → numbers. Practice writing 2–3 short, metrics-driven case statements for each project.

## 90-Day Actionable Plan (One Focused Theme: Health-Data Pipeline + Productization)

### Week 0 — Decide & Align

- Choose the theme and write a 1-sentence mission (e.g., *“Make a deployable pipeline that turns phone photos of BP monitors into actionable clinic alerts”*).  
- Set 3 measurable outcomes:  
  - M1: Working OCR → Firestore ingestion  
  - M2: Simple anomaly detector  
  - M3: Pilot case study & 1 short public writeup  

### Weeks -1 to 4 — Build the Ingestion MVP

- **Day-to-day**: Collect 300–500 labeled BP images (your phone + colleagues).  
- **Build OCR pipeline** (local proof) → Dockerize → push to Cloud Function / small server.  
- Hook Firestore or BigQuery for structured storage.  
- **Deliverable**: Working end-to-end demo and README.  

### Weeks 5–8 — Analytics + CI/CD + Privacy Checklist

- Add simple analytics: rolling averages, outlier detector (statistical or Light ML).  
- Add automated tests and a simple CI (GitHub Actions / Cloud Build).  
- Draft a privacy checklist for clinic pilot (data minimization, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logs).  
- **Deliverable**: Deployable pipeline + automated deploy, pilot checklist.  

### Weeks 9–12 — Pilot & Narrative

- Run a 2-week pilot with one clinic or a clinician friend. Collect feedback & metrics (ingestion accuracy, time saved).  
- Prepare one 15-minute demo + a 1-page case study for your portfolio / QBR.  
- **Deliverable**: Pilot report + social post + updated resume bullet.  

## Daily / Weekly Habits (Practical, Measurable)

### Technical Habits

- **Daily 60–90 min focused block** (Pomodoro 25/5 or 50/10) for “deep work” on your 90-day theme. Track completion in a simple habit tracker.  
- **Weekly deploy day**: Every Friday, merge one PR and deploy — even if small. Shipping > polishing.  
- **Biweekly public artifact**: Publish a small demo, blog, or a short tech video twice a month.  

### Music/Creative Habits

- **Daily 30–45 min “ear and mix” session**: Pick a reference track and do A/B comparison for 15 minutes (balance + stereo image), then 15–30 minutes applying one change to your project (panning, transients, saturation).  
- Finish 1 track every 2 weeks (template + stems + 2 TikTok clips). Consistency matters more than perfection.  

### Physical & Recovery

- 3* strength sessions / week, 2* conditioning, 10 min daily mobility. Track sleep (aim 7–8 h) and a weekly body check (weight, how you feel).  
- Use training as a non-negotiable anchor to maintain clarity under busy weeks.  

### Career & Leadership

- **Network sprint**: 30 min/week — reach out to 3 people with a specific ask (feedback on pilot, intro to clinic, possible collaborator).  
- Write weekly 20-minute post: short technical note or case study. Helps narrative discipline and builds credibility.  

### Family & Financial Habits

- **“Family fund” habit**: Automatic transfer to a savings bucket each payday (start at a modest percent).  
- Weekly 20-minute emotional check-in with partner / potential partner practice (use prompts: wins, worries, needs).

## Mental Models & Decision Rules to Adopt

- **Quarterly theme**: Limit attention to one thematic bet per 90 days. Everything else is optional.  
- **Ship > perfect**: 2 releases > 20 drafts. Public signals compound over private perfection.  
- **Inverse prioritization**: If a task is boring but high leverage (CI, deploy scripts, templates), do that before the fun stuff — it unlocks time later.
