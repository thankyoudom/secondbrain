## Content Ideas for Future Posts

- **Behind-the-scenes**: Show your workflow from scratch to final track

- **Genre-specific content**: Focus on your preferred style (e.g., lo-fi, trap, boom bap) to build a consistent identity

- **Process storytelling**: Narrate your creative journey — what inspires you, how you overcome challenges

## Video Tips

- Keep videos under 60 seconds for better retention
- Use text overlays to highlight key points (e.g., "This is my first lo-fi beat!")
- Show your screen or hands to build authenticity
- Maintain consistent branding: use the same font, colors, or intro/outro

## Next Steps

- Define your music style and tools to create a consistent content theme
