# Salman

## Background & Education

Mohammed bin Salman (MBS) is the Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia and chairs both the Public Investment Fund (PIF) and the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).  
He studied law and political science domestically and has no formal Western university credentials. His rapid rise in the royal hierarchy followed senior appointments in the Riyadh governorate and Ministry of Defense.

## Daily Routine & Habits

Concrete details about his personal daily habits are limited. Public profiles, such as those in *TIME*, depict him as highly driven—working long hours, commanding meetings, and personally overseeing megaprojects like NEOM and large-scale AI initiatives (*THE MENA*).  
While the anecdote of reading one book per week circulates, there is no direct confirmation in mainstream sources. His leadership of academic and innovation foundations—including the Misk Foundation, Nonprofit City, and Ilmi Centre—signals a strong commitment to knowledge empowerment and education (*Wikipedia*).

## Tech & AI Focus

### AI Leadership & Strategy

In May 2025, MBS launched HUMAIN, a national AI company under PIF, aiming to build cloud infrastructure, data centers, and multimodal Arabic-language large language models (LLMs). He chairs HUMAIN directly, demonstrating a personal commitment to establishing Saudi Arabia as a global AI hub (*Wikipedia*).  
Saudi Arabia’s National Strategy for Data & AI (approved July 2020) targets a top-15 global ranking in AI by 2030, with plans to train 20,000+ specialists and attract approximately 75 billion SAR in investments (*SDAIA*).

### Broader Tech Investments

In February 2024, Alat was founded—a PIF-owned industrial technology conglomerate focused on semiconductors, smart devices, and infrastructure—projected to contribute billions to GDP by 2030 (*Wikipedia*).  
MBS also supports the Prince Mohammed Bin Salman College for Cybersecurity & AI, Saudi Arabia’s first dedicated institution with international partnerships (Stanford, Carnegie Mellon, etc.) (*Wikipedia*).

## Vision to 2050 & Near-Future Plans

His core framework is Vision 2030, which includes investments in AI, renewable energy, tourism, and entertainment to reduce oil dependency. Projects such as NEOM, the Red Sea Project, and Qiddiya are key pillars (*THE MENA*).  
Through HUMAIN and Alat, the goal is to position Saudi Arabia as a leader in AI and advanced manufacturing by 2030. Long-term, MBS envisions the Kingdom as a global innovation leader beyond 2050. Analysts note ambitions comparable to lunar or Mars-scale endeavors in tech and science (*TIME*).  
MBS is also shaping Saudi Arabia’s global digital diplomacy—hosting international AI summits (e.g., September 2025) and chairing strategic coordination through entities like the Digital Cooperation Organization (*Saudi Press*).

## Personal Side & Leadership Style

Observers describe MBS as a modernizer—tech-savvy, informal, and aggressive in reform. However, he is also hard-edged, consolidating power through anti-corruption purges and facing criticism for human rights issues, such as the Khashoggi assassination (*TIME*).  
His diplomatic approach is pragmatic, balancing ties with the U.S., China, and Russia to pursue a "Saudi-first" agenda—seeking strategic autonomy rather than dependence on any single bloc (*TIME*).

## Summary

MBS clearly prioritizes AI and technology, embedding them structurally into national transformation.  
While personal habits like reading one book per week remain anecdotal, they align with a broader pattern of institutional support for education and innovation.  
His vision extends into the mid-century, aiming to build human capital, infrastructure, and global prestige in high-tech and scientific fields.
