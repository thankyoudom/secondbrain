# Gaming: Free-to-Play MMOs and Alternatives to RuneScape

## MMORPGs with Free Play

- **RuneScape 3 & Old School RuneScape (OSRS)**  
  Still going strong. Both are free to jump into. OSRS in particular has a huge community and regular updates. If you want that exact vibe, this is the safest bet.

- **Albion Online**  
  Very RuneScape-esque in its sandbox style. You can gather, craft, trade, PvE, or PvP. Fully free-to-play, cross-platform, and the economy is player-driven.

- **Trove**  
  More cartoony/voxel-based, but has that same "do anything" feel with building, crafting, and adventuring.

- **AdventureQuest 3D**  
  A modernized version of the old browser game. More lightweight but scratches the casual MMO itch.

## Free Sandbox / Social Games

- **Wurm Online**  
  Survival crafting MMO (the game Notch worked on before Minecraft). Slow-paced but very RuneScape-y in skills and grinding.

- **Project: Gorgon**  
  Quirky, old-school-style MMO where you can literally learn oddball skills like cheese-making. Free demo, small but dedicated community.

- **BitCraft (in testing)**  
  Upcoming sandbox MMO that many people compare to RuneScape in ambition. Worth keeping on your radar.

## If You Liked the “Level Everything” Grind

- **Villagers & Heroes**  
  Free MMO with a focus on gathering, crafting, and casual play.

- **Lost Ark**  
  More action-combat, but still F2P with lots of progression and exploration.

## Browser / Low-Spec Options (like OG RuneScape was)

- **Muck / Survive.io / Browser RPGs on Itch.io**  
  If you want quick and light browser-based survival/RPG experiences.

- **Genfanad**  
  A modern indie MMORPG that’s explicitly inspired by RuneScape.

## Key Takeaways

- If you liked RuneScape mainly for the social + chill grind, **Albion Online** and **OSRS** are probably your best bets today.  
- If you liked it for the exploration + quirky skills, check out **Project: Gorgon** or **Villagers & Heroes**.

## Steam Free Games with RuneScape Vibes

For more "RuneScape-y" vibes, consider:

- Smaller free indie RPGs on Steam, especially those tagged with **sandbox**, **MMORPG**, or **casual RPG**.  
- Look for games with skill trees, gathering, and crafting — sometimes not all content is free, but the base game gives a lot.
