# Style: The Ruler

**Princess Sheikha Hind Bint Hamad Al Thani**

### Environment Design
The process of addressing surrounding environmental parameters when devising plans, programs, policies, buildings, or products.

**Key Attributes of the Ruler**
- **Sophistication, authority, and an air of power**
- **Rich, dark colours**: Deep hues such as navy blue, black, burgundy, and forest green convey a sense of authority and command.
- **Luxurious fabrics**: High-quality materials like silk, wool, and cashmere emphasise the importance of the wearer.
- **Tailored fits**: Well-fitted and structured garments, like blazers, suit jackets, and pencil skirts, create a polished and professional appearance.
- **Classic patterns**: Subtle patterns like pinstripes, herringbone, or plaid add visual interest while maintaining the style’s sophisticated appeal.
- **Statement accessories**: Bold jewelry, such as a statement necklace, cufflinks, or a luxury watch, can be used to accentuate the Ruler archetype.
- **Formal footwear**: Elegant shoes, like leather oxfords, brogues, or high-heeled pumps, complement the overall look.
- **Power pieces**: Items that denote status, such as a tailored suit, a designer handbag, or a well-made coat, reinforce the Ruler archetype’s commanding presence.

# Color Psychology: Green

**Green (Secondary Color)**
- Green is a down-to-earth color that represents new beginnings, growth, renewal, and abundance.
- It also carries negative associations such as envy or jealousy and a lack of experience.

**Green in Color Psychology**
- Strongly connected to nature and money.
- Positive meanings: growth, fertility, health, and generosity.
- Negative associations: envy.

# The C-Suite

This classification of roles covers high-ranking executives within an organization. The ‘C’ in C-suite stands for chief. There’s a trend for the C-suite to build data-driven decision-making into their processes. Individuals in these leadership roles are expected to be familiar with data and analytics.

**Key Attributes**
- **What they do**: Responsible for data and data professionals across an entire organization.
- **How they do it**: Decision-makers at the top of a company’s hierarchy.
- **Examples of job titles**:
  - Chief Technology Officer
  - Chief Marketing Officer
  - Chief Data Officer
  - Chief Analytics Officer
  - Chief Information Officer
  - Chief Data Scientist

# Halal Hobbies in Islam

Islam encourages individuals to engage in beneficial, constructive, and Islamic-principles-based activities. Here are some permissible hobbies:

- **Sports and physical activities**: Swimming, running, cycling, soccer, basketball, martial arts, or any activity promoting health and discipline.
- **Reading and learning**: Pursuing knowledge through books, articles, or studying beneficial subjects.
- **Writing**: Poetry, fiction, non-fiction, or creative writing.
- **Gardening**: Cultivating plants, flowers, or vegetables to connect with nature and appreciate Allah’s creation.
- **Cooking and culinary arts**: Preparing food, exploring cuisines, and developing culinary skills.
- **Photography and painting**: Engaging in photography or painting while avoiding depictions of living beings (in painting).
- **Calligraphy**: Practicing Arabic calligraphy, a revered and permissible hobby in Islamic culture.
- **Volunteering and community service**: Helping others through charitable activities or community projects.
- **Learning and playing musical instruments**: Permissible by many scholars, especially when not explicitly prohibited.
- **Outdoor activities**: Hiking, camping, fishing, or exploring nature while maintaining modesty.

> *Note: The permissibility of a hobby may vary based on cultural norms, personal circumstances, and individual interpretations. Muslims are encouraged to seek knowledge, consult scholars, and engage in activities that promote personal growth and positive values.*

# The Magician

**Style Characteristics**
- **An air of mystique and sophisticated minimalism**
- **Rich, dark or light colours**: Deep hues like navy, burgundy, and emerald green, or metallic shades such as gold and silver, evoke power and enchantment.
- **Luxurious fabrics**: Materials like silk, velvet, and brocade contribute to a sumptuous and elegant feel.
- **Dramatic cuts**: Asymmetrical designs, flowing capes or sleeves, and high collars create an air of mystique and theatricality.
- **Futuristic patterns and tech-inspired elements**: Geometric shapes, holographic or metallic materials, and innovative textiles add a modern, cutting-edge appeal.
- **Layering**: Layered garments and accessories, like scarves, shawls, or vests, provide depth and complexity.
- **Bold accessories**: Statement jewellery such as large rings, pendant necklaces, or elaborate headpieces emphasize the Magician’s mystical allure.
- **Distinctive footwear**: Heeled boots, ornate sandals, or eye-catching shoes complement the overall style.

# Life Path Number Calculation

To determine the Life Path Number, add the digits of your full birthdate and reduce to a single digit.

**Example: March 20, 1996**
1. Full birthdate: 03/20/1996
2. Sum of digits: 0 + 3 + 2 + 0 + 1 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 6 = 39
3. Reduce: 3 + 9 = 12 → 1 + 2 = 3

**Life Path Number 3: Characteristics**
- **Creative and Expressive**: A strong sense of creativity; enjoys expressing themselves through writing, art, or performance.
- **Optimistic and Enthusiastic**: Positive outlook and infectious enthusiasm that inspires others.
- **Social and Charismatic**: Outgoing, enjoys socializing, and often becomes the center of attention.
- **Imaginative**: Vivid imagination and a unique way of perceiving the world.
- **Struggle with Focus**: Tends to scatter energy and interests, making sustained focus challenging.
