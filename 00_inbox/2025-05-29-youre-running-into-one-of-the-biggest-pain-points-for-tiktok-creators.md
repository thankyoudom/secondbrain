# You're running into one of the biggest pain points for TikTok creators…

You're running into one of the biggest pain points for TikTok creators: audio compression. TikTok degrades sound quality, especially when uploading music embedded in videos from editing apps like CapCut. The “Import Sound” feature offers limited relief, as it still compresses audio and can flatten your mix.

To preserve sound quality and make your beats easier to reuse (which boosts virality), your instinct is correct: distribute your audio as a sound via an official distributor.

## Best Free Distributor for TikTok Sounds (Unlimited Uploads)

**Routenote**  
- Website: [routenote.com](https://routenote.com)  
- Free Tier: 100% free distribution (they take 15% of royalties; you keep 85%)  
- Supports: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts  
- Unlimited uploads  
- Ideal for beatmakers, lo-fi producers, sample loops, etc.

### How It Works

1. **Create an Account**  
   Sign up for free. Verify your identity.  

2. **Upload Your Track**  
   - Choose the Free Distribution option.  
   - Upload in .WAV or .MP3 (preferably high-quality .WAV: 16-bit or 24-bit, 44.1kHz).  
   - Fill in metadata: artist name, track title, genre, etc.  

3. **Select Stores**  
   Ensure TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Content ID, and other platforms are selected.  

4. **Wait for Approval**  
   Approval takes 1–3 weeks (depending on volume and quality control).  

5. **Use Your Sound in TikTok Videos**  
   - Search for your artist name or track title on TikTok.  
   - Select the sound and build content around it.  
   - Encourage reuse by artists, dancers, sketch creators, etc.

### Pro Tips

- Create short, hooky, loopable beats (15–30 seconds ideal).  
- Add artist tags in the sound title, e.g., *Sunset Skies (prod. @yourhandle)*.  
- Encourage art creators to use your sound — this spreads organically in your niche.  
- Drop visuals and track previews on your TikTok while waiting for distribution to go live (build anticipation).  
- Later, upload full beats to streaming platforms like Spotify via Routenote.

## Other Free TikTok Distribution Options

- **SoundOn (by TikTok)** – Fastest free option (see below).  
- **DistroKid** – Paid, but fast (see below).  
- **DIY Workaround** – See below.

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Rely on uploading beats inside videos unless you have no choice. Audio will always compress.  
- ❌ Over-EQ or over-master — TikTok compression harshens bright highs and sub-bass.  
- ❌ Re-upload TikTok-exported videos back into CapCut — each re-upload degrades quality.

## Fastest Ways to Get High-Quality Audio on TikTok (Without Waiting Weeks)

### 1. Use SoundOn (by TikTok) — Fastest Free Option

**Website:** [soundon.global](https://soundon.global)  
- Owned by TikTok  
- Free + 100% royalties  
- Uploads often go live in 24–72 hours  
- Direct route into TikTok’s sound library  

### How It Works

1. Sign up and verify (can take a day).  
2. Upload your track (WAV/MP3).  
3. Choose TikTok and other platforms.  
4. Wait for approval — usually within 2–3 days.  
5. Your sound appears in TikTok’s official sound library.  
6. **Bonus:** Since TikTok owns SoundOn, your sounds may receive better algorithmic treatment.

### 2. Distribute Through DistroKid (Paid – but Fast)

**Website:** [distrokid.com](https://distrokid.com)  
- $22.99/year for unlimited uploads  
- Uploads to TikTok within 1–3 days  
- Also gets you on Spotify, Apple Music, etc.  

**Verdict:** If you're serious about long-term growth and music monetization, this is 100% worth it. You can upload unlimited tracks and get fast distribution across platforms.

### 3. DIY Workaround (Less Clean, Still Useful)

If you're in a time crunch and need something *today*:  
- Post your beat video privately or unlisted on TikTok with clean audio.  
- Use that post’s sound in your public content.  
- Pin it or save it for reuse.  

> **Limitation:** Your audio won’t appear in the official sound library, so virality is limited. Still a useful interim fix.
