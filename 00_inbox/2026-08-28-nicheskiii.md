# next 5 years — leverage, career & creative direction

The current priority is not to maximize creative output, followers, or platforms.

The priority is to build **leverage** while becoming more intentional about the kind of life and culture I want to participate in.

I have spent roughly two decades developing musical and visual taste. I do not need to abandon that investment, but I also do not need to force myself to become a world-class producer or illustrator.

The emerging direction is:

> **Move from primarily making things myself toward developing taste, curating, directing, building systems, and creating environments around excellent work.**

## The Primary Objective

### Secure a strong technical career position

The highest-leverage short-term objective is landing a cloud / platform / DevOps-oriented position.

This provides:

- Income
- Independence
- Professional credibility
- Technical depth
- Network
- Capital for future creative projects
- Freedom to experiment without needing immediate creative income

The career should be treated as the **economic foundation**, not as abandoning the creative side.

## The Secondary Objective

### Develop cultural depth

Rather than immediately building a music business, spend time becoming more deeply educated in:

- Jazz
- Jazz history
- Listening culture
- Hi-fi
- Japanese café / listening-room culture
- Architecture and atmosphere
- Art and design
- Game music
- Interactive media
- Creative technology

The objective is not credentials or expertise for its own sake.

The objective is:

> **Develop a deeper understanding of what kind of culture, spaces, sounds, and people I actually want around me.**

## Important Reframe

The question is no longer:

> "What should I build around the DJs I already know?"

The better question is:

> **"What kind of cultural environment do I want to create and participate in over the next 5–10 years?"**

This matters because some existing music environments are centered around:

- Alcohol
- Substances
- Nightlife
- Lyrics that do not align with my values
- Social environments I do not want to build my life around

That does not mean music itself is the wrong direction.

It means the **constraints need to change**.

## New Creative Constraints

Potential future creative environments should prioritize:

- Excellent sound
- Intentional listening
- Beautiful spaces
- Good conversation
- Daytime / early-evening culture
- Coffee / tea / food rather than substance-centered environments
- Music that resonates with my values
- Art and design
- Technology
- Community
- Slower, more thoughtful experiences

Shabbat can also function as a personal boundary:

> **Build intensely during the week; protect Saturday as a slower day for reading, reflection, movement, art, music, and being present.**

The goal is not to build a brand around these values necessarily.

The goal is to build a life that embodies them.

# TANX RADIO

TANX RADIO remains an interesting long-term possibility, but it should not become the immediate priority.

The original concept was:

> A curated music platform built around DJs and producers I know, with a high quality bar for music, sound, presentation, and eventually physical spaces.

The concept may now be better expressed as:

> **TANX = intentional listening and creative culture.**

Radio can be one expression of it rather than the entire identity.

Potential future expressions:

- TANX RADIO
- Curated mixes
- Jazz discovery
- Artist sessions
- Listening sessions
- Photography
- Visual art
- Writing
- Hi-fi
- Creative technology
- Interactive music experiences
- Eventually physical listening spaces

The genre does not necessarily need to be the filter.

**Quality and intentionality can be the filter.**

# The Platform Comes Later

Do not prematurely build:

- A venue
- A large artist roster
- A record label
- A social network
- A giant radio infrastructure
- A monetization strategy

First develop the underlying taste.

The platform should eventually emerge from genuine relationships, curation, and cultural understanding.

The fundamental question is:

> **Can I become someone whose taste other people trust?**

## Creative Practice

Do not abandon music or visual art.

But remove the requirement that they become businesses.

Instead:

> **Make small things consistently.**

Possible outputs:

- Procreate illustrations
- Digital art
- Music
- AI-assisted music
- Mini mixes
- Photography
- Game experiments
- Creative coding
- Music videos
- Visual studies
- Jazz-inspired studies

The goal is **continued creative fluency**, not follower accumulation.

## Why Not Chase 1K Followers Everywhere?

Potential short-term goals considered:

- 1K YouTube followers through type beats / music vlogs
- 1K Instagram followers through digital art
- 1K TikTok followers through gaming/music content
- 1K SoundCloud followers through mixes and curation

These are concrete but may not represent meaningful leverage.

The danger is creating four small jobs:

- Content creator
- Art account
- Gaming account
- Music curator

and ending up with thousands of followers but no substantial change in career, independence, network, or cultural position.

**Followers are a metric. They are not automatically leverage.**

## Better Daily Structure

### Primary — Career

**60–120 minutes, most weekdays**

Do something that directly increases the probability of landing and succeeding in a cloud/platform role.

Examples:

- Applications
- Interview preparation
- Cloud projects
- Kubernetes
- Terraform
- GCP
- Linux
- System design
- Portfolio improvement
- Networking

Goal:

> **Offer.**

### Secondary — Cultural Development

**30–45 minutes/day**

Listen, read, study, or observe.

Especially:

- Jazz
- Jazz history
- Hi-fi
- Japanese listening culture
- Design
- Games
- Creative technology

Goal:

> **Become culturally deeper, not merely more productive.**

### Tertiary — Creative Practice

**~30 minutes/day**

Make something.

No follower requirement.

No platform requirement.

No requirement that it be commercially useful.

Goal:

> **Preserve and compound creative ability.**

### Weekly — Cultural Exposure

**One intentional outing or experience**

Examples:

- Jazz performance
- Record store
- Hi-fi shop
- Café
- Library
- Gallery
- Museum
- Interesting game / interactive experience
- Beautiful public space

Ask:

> **"What kind of environment makes me want to stay?"**

This becomes research for the future.

# The Smallest TANX Experiment

Instead of launching a platform, practice **curation**.

Once per week, create something like:

> **TANX / WEEK 01**

Containing:

- 5 songs or records
- 1 visual
- 1 mix
- 1 game / interactive work
- 1 short thought

This can remain private or be published.

The point is to practice:

> **Find → evaluate → connect → sequence → present.**

That may eventually become the core skill behind TANX.

# Potential Long-Term Evolution

### 2026–2027 — Foundation

Career first.

Develop technical depth.

Study jazz and listening culture.

Maintain creative practice.

Experiment privately with TANX.

### 2027–2028 — Community

If the interest persists:

- Small listening sessions
- Guest mixes
- Collaborations
- Pop-ups
- Artist sessions
- Curated events

Use existing spaces rather than owning one.

### 2028–2030 — Creative Technology

Explore the intersection of:

- Music
- Games
- Interactive media
- Generative systems
- Sound design
- Visual environments
- Technology

Build small experiments.

Find the intersection that feels uniquely mine.

### 2030–2031 — Physical Space, If Earned

Only consider a permanent space if the community and concept already exist.

Potential concept:

> **Listening room × café × library × radio station × creative studio**

Not necessarily a nightclub.

Not necessarily a bar.

Not necessarily even a music venue.

The physical space should be the **manifestation of an existing culture**, not the thing that creates the culture.

# Core Strategic Principle

Do not try to predict exactly what Millennials and Gen Z will want in 2035.

Instead:

> **Build toward the kind of environment I personally believe should exist.**

Potential thesis:

> **Beautiful spaces. Excellent sound. Intentional listening. Interesting people. Technology used thoughtfully. Less noise. Less excess. More depth.**

If other people want the same environment, a community can form around it.

# The Five-Year Question

The goal is not to decide exactly what I will become.

The goal is to become:

> **More technically capable.  
> More financially independent.  
> More culturally literate.  
> More creatively fluent.  
> More discerning about people and environments.**

From there, the larger direction can emerge.

The current hierarchy is:

**1. Build economic independence.**

**2. Build cultural depth.**

**3. Preserve creative ability.**

**4. Practice curation.**

**5. Let TANX—or whatever comes next—emerge from those foundations.**

The deeper transition may be:

> **Producer → Engineer → Curator → Creative Director → Platform Builder → Cultural Entrepreneur**

There is no need to force the later stages.

The immediate objective is simply to build the foundation that makes them possible.
