# It sounds like you’ve already done a lot of the hard work with LaunchCode…

It sounds like you’ve already done a lot of the hard work with LaunchCode (LC) — great job on completing your coursework! I looked into the current status of the org and here’s a breakdown of what I discovered plus how you might proceed — with caveats.

## What I found that is clear

- **Headquarters**: LaunchCode is headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, at 4811 Delmar Blvd. (launchcode.org)
- **Hub areas**: They explicitly state their core programs enroll learners from three metro areas: St. Louis (MO), Kansas City (MO), and Philadelphia (PA) (codecamps.launchcode.org)
- **No South Florida hub**: There is no public mention of a South Florida hub (e.g., Boca Raton / Miami area) in their service areas. Their FAQ states: *“At this time, we are only enrolling learners from the St. Louis, Kansas City and Philadelphia metro areas into our core programs…”* (launchcode.org)
- **Mission remains active**: LaunchCode continues its mission of free tech training, paid apprenticeships, and job placement efforts (launchcode.org)

## What isn’t totally clear — and what that means for your situation

- There is no definitive public statement confirming that LaunchCode has closed operations in South Florida. However, local reports suggest they may have scaled back or shifted operations.
- Your situation is unique: you completed coursework but did not finish the capstone or placement steps. This places you in an “in-between” zone.
- Public documentation does not clarify whether returning students can resume dropped elements after a long gap — especially if outside hub areas.
- Their FAQ states that living outside the hub areas is generally a barrier: *“We are only enrolling learners from the St. Louis, Kansas City and Philadelphia metro areas…”* (launchcode.org)

## What you can do (and steps I’d suggest)

Given your background and openness to relocating (a significant positive), here’s how you might proceed:

### Reach out directly to LaunchCode

- **Email**: Use the contact address listed on their “About” page (codecamps.launchcode.org)
- **Phone**: 314-254-0107 (St. Louis HQ) — note: this is the St. Louis office (Give STL Day)

### Ask specifically

- *“I completed the coursework in your earlier South Florida offering (or via your program) but did not complete the capstone/project + job placement steps. Am I eligible to resume/complete those steps now?”*
- *“Even though I’m currently located in South Florida, am I eligible for the program given your hub-area policy? If I relocated to one of your hub cities, what are my options?”*
- *“Are there any alumni-oriented or returning-student pathways that allow me to pick up where I left off?”*

### Evaluate relocation possibility

Since you’re open to relocating, consider moving (or commuting/remote-if-allowed) to one of the hub cities: St. Louis, Kansas City, or Philadelphia. This would significantly improve your eligibility under their current policy. The key question: Does the “capstone + placement” require physical presence or regional employer connections? Ask when you contact them.

### Document your past completion

When reaching out, provide proof of your coursework completion (e.g., grades, certificates, transcripts). Clearly explain why the capstone and placement steps were not completed. This helps them determine if you’re in “good standing” and eligible to resume.

### Explore alternatives

While contacting LaunchCode, research alternative programs (bootcamps or apprenticeships) that:
- Accept learners in South Florida (or offer remote options)
- Accept students who have already completed coursework and are only missing capstone/placement steps

This ensures you have a backup plan if LaunchCode cannot accommodate you.

### Check alumni/returning-student policies

LaunchCode has an “[Alumni Committee]” (mentioned in their FAQ), which may assist you in advancing or accessing job placement — even without completing the formal capstone via LC. (codecamps.launchcode.org)
