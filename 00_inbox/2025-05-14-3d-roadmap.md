# 3D Roadmap

## Why Learn CAD in 2025?

CAD is no longer just a tool for mechanical engineers. It's a core skill across creative and technical industries—offering solid career paths and freelance opportunities.

- **Career Flexibility & Future-Proofing**  
  CAD is used in jewelry, fashion, architecture, interior design, product design, film/VFX, gaming, VR/AR, and manufacturing.  
  As 3D printing, digital fabrication, and virtual environments expand, CAD skills become increasingly valuable.  
  Remote-friendly: Once skilled, you can freelance from anywhere.

## CAD in Jewelry Design

### CAD is heavily used in:
- Ring design  
- Custom grillz  
- Tooth gems  
- Chains, pendants, earrings  
- Luxury and high-end prototyping  

Popular programs include:
- **Rhino** – Industry standard for jewelry  
- **MatrixGold** – Specialized for jewelry design  
- **ZBrush** – For organic sculpting  

These tools enable you to design intricate pieces for:
- 3D printing for casting  
- Custom client work  
- Selling STL files (digital product income)

## How Long to Learn from Zero?

With consistent practice (4–8 hours/week) using tutorials and projects, you can reach client-readiness in **6–12 months**.

## Salaries & Income Potential

Modern monetization routes include:
- Selling STL files  
- Taking custom design commissions  
- Creating NFTs or digital assets  

## Industries & Serious Career Paths

Without a mechanical engineering background, here are real opportunities:

### Jewelry & Fashion Design
- In-house or freelance CAD designer  
- Work with casting houses or luxury brands  
- Build your own brand or Etsy shop  

### Product Design
- Freelance prototyping for consumer goods  
- 3D printing-focused design studios  

### Entertainment & Media
- Model props/jewelry for game or film assets  
- Work in 3D environments for AR/VR filters or assets  

### Dental & Medical Modeling
- High-end work: grills, crowns, custom tooth accessories  
- Often combines CAD with 3D scanning  

## What You Can Start With (Free or Cheap Tools)

- **Blender** – Free, powerful for modeling and rendering  
- **Fusion 360** – Free for personal/hobby use; great for mechanical and organic design  
- **Rhino 3D** – Paid, but industry standard for jewelry (look for student/demo)  
- **Tinkercad** – Super basic but ideal for learning fundamentals  
- **ZBrushCoreMini** – Free sculpting, excellent for grill/tooth gem modeling  

## Pathway You Could Take

1. Learn Blender (basic modeling + rendering)  
2. Move into Fusion 360 or Rhino for precision and real-world applications  
3. Start modeling rings, grills, pendants  
4. Learn how to export for 3D printing/casting  
5. Build a small portfolio or online store  
6. Network with dental techs, jewelers, or fashion artists  
7. Scale into freelance or in-house design roles  

## Final Thoughts

You don’t need a mechanical background—just a strong design sense, patience, and comfort with 3D space. CAD can turn your creative ideas into tangible, profitable results. With the right path, you can grow into other industries as you progress.
