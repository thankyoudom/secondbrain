## Tsuruda-Inspired Mixing Blueprint

### Leveling Structure (Before Mastering)
- Master output peak should be around -6 dB for headroom.
- Use a peak meter or LUFS short-term meter (e.g., Youlean or Span) to monitor gain staging at transient peaks.

### Panning & Width
- Tsuruda-style tracks breathe in the stereo field.
- Use a stereo imager (e.g., Ozone Imager, FL Stereo Shaper) to visualize width.
- Always check Mono Compatibility before mastering.

### Frequency Zones (Clarity is Key)
- Break your mix into frequency bands to avoid cramming multiple elements into the same space.

### Mixing Sauce for a Full, Tsuruda-Style Sound
1. **Parallel Compression (Drums Bus)**
   - Duplicate the drums to a bus.
   - Compress with ratio 10:1, fast attack, medium release.
   - Mix in at 10–30% for punch without over-limiting.

2. **Saturation (Tastefully)**
   - **Drums**: Use tape or analog-style saturation (e.g., Decapitator, Saturn, RC-20).
   - **Bass**: Add subtle drive for harmonics.
   - **Master Bus**: Apply light glue saturation (e.g., Kramer Tape, Black Box).

3. **Reverb Sends Instead of Inserts**
   - Create one or two main reverb buses.
   - Send melody, FX, and perc to them with varying send levels.
   - Keeps space unified instead of letting each sound float independently.

4. **Sidechain Ducking**
   - Duck bass, pads, reverb returns, and background percussions.
   - Keeps drums central without overpowering.

### Final Tips
- **Highpass ruthlessly** – even FX and melodies need clean low ends.
- **Automate volume and width** – small shifts in loop sections create dynamic growth.
- **Compare to references** – level-match "Picasso" and Ivy Lab tracks at equal loudness.
- **Bus your groups** – drums, melodic, FX, vocals – process in chunks.

## Execution Plan – High Quality Mix (Ivy Lab / Tsuruda Vibe)

### 1. Session Structure
- Color-code and name all tracks.
- Use buses: Drums, Melodic, Sub/Bass, FX, Master Bus.
- Route every group to its own mixer track with headroom.

### 2. Sub Bass (Your DX10)
#### Sound Design Checklist
- **DX10 Sine**: EQ out highs (lowpass @ ~100–150 Hz).
- If too clean: Layer with a faint, distorted sub.
- Use 3x Osc with triangle or square, slightly detuned.
- Route to the same Sub Bus.

#### Best FREE FX Chain for “Fuzzy, Analog” Sub
- Pro Tip: Use Automation Clips to bring in fuzz only on drops or fill moments.

### 3. Sub Movement Tips (Ivy Lab Style)
- **Pitch Slides**: Use glide/portamento in DX10 or 3x Osc for slippery pitch moments.
- **Sidechain Sub to Kick**: Clean duck using Peak Controller or Fruity Limiter.
- **Layering Texture**: Take a low sine and layer noise/grain only on note start with envelope control.

### Mixing Workflow Summary
- **Sound Select**: Start with clean, simple sounds you can dirty later.
- **Group Early**: Route kicks, subs, melodies, FX to buses for control.
- **Saturate Subtly**: Focus on tone shaping, not full-on crush.
- **Layer with Intention**: Build width & fuzz using highs only—not muddy mids.
- **Reference Constantly**: Level-match "Picasso" and Ivy Lab tracks while mixing.
