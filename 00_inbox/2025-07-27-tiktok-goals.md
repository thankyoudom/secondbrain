# TikTok Goals

You're absolutely right — TikTok has unofficial "levels" or benchmarks that many creators observe as their accounts grow. These aren't officially labeled by TikTok as "levels," but based on community experience and TikTok’s own unlockable features, certain milestones do open up new tools and opportunities.

## Follower-Based Benchmarks

- **Website Link in Bio**:  
  You need to meet both:  
  - 1,000 followers  
  - Switch to a Business Account  

  ### Steps to Add a Link  
  1. Go to Profile > Manage Account > Switch to Business Account  
  2. Choose a category (like "Creator" or "Personal Blog")  
  3. Once you hit 1,000 followers, a Website field will appear under your bio settings  

## Like-Based Benchmarks

- Likes are cumulative across all videos — not per video.
