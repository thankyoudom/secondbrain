# Rnb

## Remix Submission Platforms & Curators

### Digiwaxx Media (Hip-Hop & R&B)
A reputable campaign platform with frequent remix and beat contests.  
- Post and tag on TikTok/Instagram (e.g., @IamCarolDoche, @Digiwaxx).  
- Submit via their website: [digiwaxx.com](https://digiwaxx.com) or [help.labelradar.com](https://help.labelradar.com).  
Ideal for gaining exposure through DJ networks, blogs, and social channels.

### Artist Influence (SoundCloud Repost Network)
- Over 50 million potential followers across genre-specific groups (e.g., Beats, R&B).  
- Free monthly submissions; repost to curate genre communities.  
- Access via: [artist.tools](https://artist.tools) or [artistinfluence.com](https://artistinfluence.com).  
Good for boosting plays on SoundCloud and building momentum.

### Spotify Playlist Curators
Use tools like [Artist.tools](https://artist.tools) to find R&B-focused playlists:  
- Example: “RnB ?” (27k followers, email contact available)  
- Example: “Rnb, Chill – Soulrise” (26k)  
Submit via email for playlist consideration.

### PlaylistPartner (Remix + Electronic Focus)
- Free submissions to remix and electronic playlists—often open to R&B remixes.  
- Website: [playlistpartner.com](https://playlistpartner.com)  
A good way to land on curated playlists, especially on Spotify.

## Legal & Platform Best Practices
- Use acapella stems and remix within copyright rules.  
- Avoid uploading to Spotify without permission.  
- Stick to SoundCloud, Mixcloud, TikTok with proper tagging (use [artist.tools](https://artist.tools) or [digiwaxx.com](https://digiwaxx.com)).  
- On Instagram or TikTok, activate Remix and Duet features to invite others to build upon your remix (source: [blog.hootsuite.com](https://blog.hootsuite.com)).

## Community & Collaboration Strategies
Launch your own “Remix This” challenge:  
- Post a beat drop and caption: “Stitch this with your verses.”  
- Tag small R&B vocalists or producers to build chain reactions.  
Join Instagram/TikTok groups for remix collaborations.  
As one Redditor noted:  
> “Honestly, finding ‘remix this’ reels is kinda tough right now… Best bet is checking hashtags like #RemixThis or following accounts that post open verse stuff.”  
(Source: [reddit.com](https://reddit.com), [artist.tools](https://artist.tools), [forbes.com](https://forbes.com))

Use trending hashtags:  
- #rnbremix  
- #rnbmusic  
- #hiphopremix  
- #producer  
- #beatmaker  
- #newmusic  

These tags help reach niche audiences (source: [blog.delivermytune.com](https://blog.delivermytune.com)).
